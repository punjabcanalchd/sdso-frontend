About

The Punjab Water Regulation and Development Authority (SDSO) constituted under the Punjab Water Resources Regulation and Management Act, 2020 is mandated to regulate the water resources of the state for ensuring its judicious, equitable and sustainable utilisation and management. The Authority aims to manage and regulate water usage in the state so as to achieve sustainable management of groundwater balanced with the requirements of the livelihoods of people of the state.
